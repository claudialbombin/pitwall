// methodology

Three layers.
One optimal decision.

01
Tyre Degradation Model
Weibull survival analysis fits the shape of tyre wear per compound. A Gaussian Process (Matérn 5/2 kernel) learns the non-parametric residuals — giving a full posterior distribution over lap time delta, not just a point estimate. The cliff is detected as the inflection point of d²μ*(t)/dt².
GPR + Weibull
02
Safety Car Probability
A non-homogeneous Poisson process models lap-varying deployment probability. Bayesian Beta-Binomial conjugate updates calibrate per-circuit priors from 2018–2023 FastF1 data. Duration is modelled as a Geometric distribution — the SC window is literally an American option.
Bayesian NHPP
03
Bellman Solver
Backward induction over the race horizon computes V*(s, t): the minimum expected remaining time from every possible race state. The optimal policy π*(s, t) = argmax of the Q-values. 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations validate the policy across stochastic race trajectories with antithetic variates for variance reduction.
Exact DP · MC

V*(s, t) =
  maxa ∈ A(s) [
    R(s, a)
    + Σs' P(s'|s,a) · V*(s', t+1)
  ]

// Boundary condition
V*(s, T+1) = 0  // race over

// Optimal policy
π*(s, t) = argmaxa Q*(s, a, t)

The Bellman Equation

At every lap, the solver asks: what action minimises the total expected race time from here to the finish? The answer is computed by working backwards from lap T — the Bellman equation guarantees we find the globally optimal policy, not a local approximation.

The state s encodes (lap, compound, tyre age, position, gap ahead, gap behind, pits used, SC active). The transition kernel P(s'|s,a) integrates both the tyre degradation posterior and the safety car deployment probability.

This is structurally identical to the Snell envelope for American option pricing. Pitting = exercising the option. Tyre degradation = theta decay. The safety car window = volatility.

F1 strategy
is options trading.

The mathematical structure of pit stop optimisation is not merely similar to quantitative finance — it is the same problem in a different domain. Every concept maps exactly.

This is why F1 strategy makes an unusual but powerful portfolio project: the problem is genuinely hard, the data is public, and the isomorphism to trading is precise enough to discuss in a technical interview.
